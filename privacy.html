Your privacy is important to us. The PetNudge iOS app stores all your pet data locally on your device (and in your private iCloud if you use iCloud sync); from the iOS app we do not collect, store, or share any personal information without your consent. If you use the PetNudge web app at app.petnudge.fr, your account and pet data are synced to our server, hosted by Cloudflare in the EU, and processed by the service providers listed below.

Information We Collect

PetNudge is designed with privacy in mind. Here's what you should know:

  • Pet Data: In the iOS app, all information about your pets (names, health records, vaccinations, medications, appointments) is stored locally on your device. In the web app, it is synced to our server (see "Web App" below).
  • Photos: In the iOS app, pet photos you add are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. In the web app, the photos and documents you add are stored on our server with the rest of your data.
  • Usage Data: We do not collect analytics or usage data about how you use the iOS app.
  • Website analytics: our website petnudge.fr uses Plausible Analytics, self-hosted by us, without cookies: it counts page views, referrers, country and device type, and clicks on outbound links (such as the App Store or the web app), with no personal data and no cross-site tracking. The iOS app and the web app (app.petnudge.fr) have no analytics. Notre site petnudge.fr utilise Plausible Analytics, hébergé par nos soins, sans cookies : il compte les pages vues, les sites de provenance, le pays et le type d'appareil, ainsi que les clics sur les liens sortants (App Store, application web), sans données personnelles ni suivi entre sites. L'application iOS et l'application web n'utilisent aucun outil de mesure d'audience.

Data Storage

In the iOS app, PetNudge uses Apple's SwiftData framework to store most of your pet information securely on your device. This means:

  • Local Data: Health records, vaccinations, medications, and appointments are stored only on your device.
  • We cannot access, read, or recover your local data.
  • iCloud sync: if you are signed in to iCloud on your device, your pet data is also kept in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so it is available on your other devices. Only you can access it; we cannot.
  • Deleting the app removes the data from that device. To delete it everywhere (device and iCloud), use Settings › Delete All Data or Delete Account in the app.

Optional Account (Sign in with Apple or Google)

You can use the PetNudge iOS app without an account. The web app requires signing in with Google or Apple. If you choose to sign in:

  • What the app receives: your name, your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can hide it behind a private relay address) and an account identifier.
  • Where it is kept (iOS app): only on your device (iOS Keychain), to show your profile in the app. The iOS app does not send it to any server of ours.
  • Where it is kept (web app): our server keeps your email address and the account identifier from Google or Apple, so you can sign in and sync your data.
  • Google Sign-In is provided by Google and covered by Google's Privacy Policy; Sign in with Apple by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your account: in the iOS app, Settings › Account › Delete Account deletes your account and all your pet data on the device and in your iCloud, removes your public tag profiles, and withdraws PetNudge's access to your Google account. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for PetNudge from your Apple Account settings.

Web App (app.petnudge.fr)

The PetNudge web app at https://app.petnudge.fr works differently from the iOS app. The iOS app is unchanged: its data stays on your device and in your private iCloud, and its purchases are billed by Apple.

  • Sign-in: you sign in with Google or Apple (see "Optional Account" above).
  • Where your data is kept: your pet data, including the photos and documents you add, is synced to our server: Cloudflare Workers, with the database in Cloudflare D1 under EU jurisdiction.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe (see "Purchases and Payments" below).
  • AI assistant: requests are processed by Google Vertex AI in the EU region (see "AI Pet Assistant" below).
  • Reminders: sent as web notifications through your browser's push service, only if you allow notifications (see "Notifications" below).
  • Deleting your account: deleting your account in the web app deletes your data on our server and also cancels your web subscription at Stripe.

Pet ID Profiles (NFC)

When you create a Pet ID Tag (NFC tag) in the iOS app, a public profile is stored in Apple's CloudKit:

  • What is stored: Pet name, species, breed, photo, owner name, and contact phone number.
  • What is NOT stored: Health records, vaccinations, medications, or appointments.
  • Purpose: Allows anyone who scans your pet's tag to see the profile and contact you.
  • Control: You can delete the profile at any time from the app.
  • Lost Mode: When enabled, finders see an urgent "Lost Pet" alert with optional reward information.

This data is stored on Apple's iCloud servers and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases and Payments

iOS app: Subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information for App Store purchases. All transactions are handled securely by Apple.

Web app: Subscriptions are paid through Stripe (Stripe Checkout and the Stripe customer portal). Your card data is handled by Stripe and is never stored by us; our server keeps your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and the subscription's status, to know whether it is active. Stripe's own Privacy Policy applies to the data it processes.

AI Pet Assistant

PetNudge includes an AI-powered pet assistant feature. Here's how it handles your data:

  • Conversations: Your chat messages are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate responses. We do not store conversation history on our servers.
  • Pet Information: Basic pet information (name, species, breed, age) may be included in AI requests to provide personalized responses.
  • Web app: AI requests from the web app go through our server to Google Vertex AI and are processed in the EU region. Each user can make at most 30 AI requests per day. We do not use your requests for training.
  • No Medical Records: Your pet's detailed health records, vaccinations, and medications are NOT sent to the AI service.
  • Disclaimer: The AI provides general information only and is NOT a substitute for professional veterinary advice. We are not veterinarians.

Third-Party Services

PetNudge uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple CloudKit: For storing public Pet ID profiles (when you create NFC tags). See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini AI: For the AI Pet Assistant feature. Conversations are processed by Google's servers. See Google's AI Terms.
  • Apple Maps: For finding veterinary clinics near you (only when you use the clinic search feature).
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In: In the iOS app, only if you choose to sign in; the web app requires it. See the "Optional Account" section above.
  • StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases in the iOS app.
  • Push Notifications: For medication and appointment reminders (only if you enable notifications).
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, API and database for the web app, in the EU (Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 under EU jurisdiction).
  • Stripe: Payments on the web app. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Vertex AI: The AI Pet Assistant in the web app. Requests are processed in the EU region, at most 30 per user per day, and are not used by us for training.
  • Your browser's push service: Reminders in the web app, only if you allow notifications in your browser.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, PetNudge will send local notifications for:

  • Medication reminders
  • Vaccination due dates
  • Upcoming vet appointments

In the iOS app, these notifications are generated locally on your device and do not require sending your data to any server. In the web app, reminders are sent as web notifications through your browser's push service, and only if you allow notifications in your browser.

Security

The PetNudge iOS app supports biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) to protect your pet's data. When enabled:

  • The app requires authentication each time it is opened.
  • Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never accessible to our app.

Children's Privacy

PetNudge does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed to track pet health information, not personal user data.

Your Rights (GDPR / RGPD)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data. In the iOS app, Settings › Delete All Data (or Delete Account if you signed in) erases your data on the device and in your iCloud and removes your public NFC Pet ID profiles. In the web app, deleting your account deletes your data on our server and also cancels your web subscription at Stripe.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format. PetNudge allows you to export health reports as PDF from within the app.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your personal data at any time.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we limit how we use your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contactpetnudge@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Data Protection Authority: If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Data Retention

The PetNudge iOS app stores your data on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database, so retention is entirely in your control: Settings › Delete All Data or Delete Account erases it everywhere, and NFC Pet ID profiles stored in CloudKit can be deleted at any time from within the app.

In the web app, we keep your data on our server while your account exists. Deleting your account in the web app deletes your data on our server and cancels your web subscription at Stripe. Payment records held by Stripe are covered by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
