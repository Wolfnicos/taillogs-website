Tag code generator

Internal tool. Generates unique codes to print on physical tags. Each code self-registers when a customer activates it in the PetNudge app — no upload needed.

#CodeTag URL (print this as a QR)

Alphabet excludes ambiguous characters (no O/0/I/1). Codes are 8 chars → 32⁸ ≈ 1.1×10¹² combinations, so collisions are effectively impossible. Feed the Tag URL column to your tag printer / QR service. Customers scan the printed QR in-app to link the tag to their pet, or the QR opens petnudge.fr/t/<CODE> directly once activated.
